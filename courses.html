Questions

Before you start

I don't know a single letter. Is that OK?

Yes. Every course is designed for complete beginners. The Reading course starts with the alphabet.

Which course should I take first?

If you can't read Arabic script yet, start with Reading. It makes Quranic Arabic and Conversation much easier.

How much time do I need?

About two to three hours a week, including class time and short practice sessions, is enough to see steady progress.
